## Issue 1. Reaction card still vanilla

**Cause: INFERRED, and the in-game probes are what will confirm it.** The failure itself is proven. The movie returned `no match` for `Sam Coe liked that.` while the JS harness matches that line and the card was on.

**PROVEN**

- The shipped name pattern used `\u`. JPEXS FFDec `-importScript` does not expand `\u` in a regex literal. The compiled HUDMessagesMenu pcode still contains a literal `\u00C0-\u024F` (research note, local pcode). A string escape is different: FFDec does expand those, so the self-test writes the regex literal `/\u0041/` (one backslash in the source) and `new RegExp("\\u0041")` (the string value is `\u0041`).
- Scaleform 4.2 uses PCRE for AS3 regex. Autodesk, *Upgrading from Scaleform 4.0 to 4.2+*: https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Scaleform-Help/scaleform_help/migration_guide/migration_upgrading.html . Public `GFxConfig.h` has `SF_ENABLE_PCRE`.
- Stock PCRE rejects `\u` unless `PCRE_JAVASCRIPT_COMPAT` is set. pcrecompat(3): https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man3/pcrecompat.3.html . Adobe's avmplus `pcre/pcre_compile.cpp` maps `\u` to error 37. `eval/eval-lex.cpp` says "PCRE does not understand \u sequences, so expand legal ones here."
- In avmplus, a failed compile stores a NULL program and `test` reports no match, with no throw (`core/RegExpObject.cpp`). Whether GFx does the same is **NOT FOUND** (GFx source is not public).
- `hzcRxOffer` turns a throw or a null into `no match`. The `i` flag only folds A-Z (AIR SDK flags page). `\x` is safe: vanilla uses `/[\x00-\x1F]/`. Named groups in AS3 are `(?P<name>)`, not `(?<`. `\p{` and a `u` flag are not AS3.

**INFERRED**

Scaleform does not expand `\u`, FFDec leaves it in the pattern, and a dead name regex makes every name fail, including pure ASCII. That fits the log. It is not proven by running Scaleform in this session. The startup self-test logs three probes even with `Debug=0`:

- `/\u0041/.test('A')`
- `new RegExp('\\u0041').test('A')`
- `/\x41/.test('A')`

Pass on the first line means `hzcRxMatch("Sam Coe liked that.")` returns Sam Coe, liked (index 1).

**Change**

The matcher no longer uses `RegExp`. Letters are A-Z, a-z, or U+00C0 through U+024F via `charCodeAt`. Apostrophes are U+0027 and U+2019. The hyphen is U+002D. The same trim covers tab, CR, LF, VT, FF, space, U+00A0, and U+FEFF. ASCII regexes that do not use `\u` stay (section trims, the optional-text checks, the reaction-log newline flatten).

**Cause of the 0.42.0.7 look: PROVEN from that source.** Height was 39, the key was nearly the full height and left-aligned in a padded field, the sweep had a 0.62 hot band, the fill was 0.84, and the glow was 0.32 at blur 10.

**PROVEN**

- `option_b()` in the mock is 1440p framebuffer pixels, not stage units: height 52, capsule radius half the height, key about 36 tall, left inset 8, gap 14, EXIT semibold 24 with 0.1 tracking, fill 0.88, idle white outline 0.18 at 1.5 px, hold outline 2 px plus a 0.30 glow, sweep 0.30 with a short fade, complete green `#5EE3A1`.
- The key cap the user asked for is about 30x30 with radius 5, which overrides the mock function's radius 6. The color is the F11 HUD accent (`HZ_ACCENT` / `hzAccentOf`), not a fixed cyan. Complete stays `HZ_GREEN` (`0x5EE3A1`).
- Vanilla `HUDVehicle.ProcessUserEvent` forwards exit only `if (visible && GetUpButton_mc != null)`, and HUDMenu sets `noInvisibleAdvance = true` (vanilla `HUDMenu.as` in Creation-Hub/Interface, commit `8b20208`). The hide sets `alpha` only. Name, x, y, and `visible` are not written.

**INFERRED**

The repo's stage note says one screen pixel is 0.75 stage units at 1440p (`1080/ScreenHeight`). The user's 48 px pill from a 39-unit box is about 1.23 screen pixels per stage unit. The new stage constants use that measured scale, not 0.75: height 42 (about 52 px), key box 24 (about 30 px), corner radius 4 stage units (about 5 px), inset 8, gap 11, key 17 bold, EXIT 19.5 semibold with `letterSpacing` of `round(size * 0.1 * 10) / 10`. Fill is `0.88 * opacity`. Idle outline is white at 0.18, stroke 1.5. Hold and complete use stroke 2. Glow is `GlowFilter` at 0.30, blur 6 (under the 15.9 px text-glow cap). The sweep is flat 0.30, then fades to 0 over 6 px. No 0.62 peak. The 13 px floor is `hz42VehFs`. Controller glyphs keep the game font and use the same accent or green. The key cap height stays 24. It does not grow with the text field's line height. Width still grows for a long glyph.

This geometry is not remeasured in game.

## Issue 3. Hold-breath chip clipped on the right while held

**Cause: INFERRED.** The harness already kept strokes inside the chip, and the chip width does not change with state. "Only while held" fits the longer label `HOLDING BREATH` and the extra 2 px hold stroke. `textWidth` often misses the last glyph's right bearing, and `letterSpacing` adds to that. `getBounds` ignores filters and fully transparent pixels.

**PROVEN**

- Scaleform caps text glow blur at 15.9 px (Autodesk, *Font Part 5*). An ancestor `scrollRect` or mask crops filter ink (Adobe `DisplayObject`). The chip has no `GlowFilter`. Adding one would not be a reliable fix.
- The chip is a child of the ScopeMenu root, not the button. `drawRoundRect(-` is still absent, so the chip does not draw outside its own box.

**Change**

Pad of 8 (`HZP_GLOW`) with a 0.01 black fill so the pad is inside `getBounds`. Label field gets 32 extra (`HZP_LABEX`, was 16) so the held card is slightly longer. The chip clears its own `scrollRect`, mask, and `cacheAsBitmap`. Up to six parents: an existing `scrollRect` grows on the right and bottom only, and its x and y stay. A non-round mask (width over 20 and `|w-h| >= 4`) may be widened once, by at most the pad plus 1. A round mask is left alone (that is the scope reticle). Ancestor `cacheAsBitmap` is not cleared. F11's preview uses the same label extra (`HZ42_SC_LABEX`). The in-game clip is still unproven. The first HOLDING BREATH of a session stores chip `getBounds(stage)`, the label `textWidth` and `width`, each ancestor's name, scrollRect, mask, scale, and `cacheAsBitmap`, plus stage scale and HudScale. The DLL logs that once as `scope holdbreath diag:`, even when `Debug=0`.

## Issue 4. Contusions subtitle shows `$1 $2`

**Cause: PROVEN for the mechanism. The on-screen letters match the literal replacement.**

Autodesk's AS3 support table marks `String.replace` **Partial: "Special replacement codes not supported"** (https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Scaleform-Help/scaleform_help/flash_support/as3_class.html , row `String.replace`). `$1`, `$2`, `$&`, `$$`, `` $` ``, and `$'` are those codes. A function as the second argument is **NOT FOUND** in that table (it only calls out the codes). No shipped movie passed a function. The harness rejects one anyway.

The only such call in the live HUD was:

```
s.replace(/([a-z])([A-Z])/g, "$1 $2")
```

inside `hzPretty`, after stripping `PersonalEffect_` / `Personal_` / `HazardEffect_` / `Hazard_` and a `_(Negative|Positive)` suffix, and turning `_` into spaces.

**What the screenshot is**

A 4x crop of the user's 2560x1440 shot reads `Cardi$1 $2espirator$1 $2irculatory`. That is exactly what a replace does when it consumes each `([a-z])([A-Z])` pair and writes the six characters `$1 $2` instead of expanding them:

| Input | Scaleform (codes left literal) | Flash (codes expanded) |
|---|---|---|
| `CardioRespiratoryCirculatory` | `Cardi$1 $2espirator$1 $2irculatory` | `Cardio Respiratory Circulatory` |

The typed line `Card$1 $2respirator$1 $2circulatory` is the same subtitle with the boundary letters easy to miss at about 10 px. It is not a second string. `CardiovascularRespiratoryCirculatory` would have left `Cardiovascula$1 $2...`, which is not what the crop shows.

**Vanilla text**

**PROVEN** from Creation-Hub/Interface @ `8b20208` (the vanilla decompile the 1022 research note already used):

- The symbol and the sound switch are `PersonalEffect_CardioRespiratoryCirculatory`, with `_Negative` / `_Positive` frame clips. The other body groups are `SkeletalMuscular`, `NervousSystem`, `DigestiveImmune`, and `Misc`.
- `BottomLeftGroup.PlayAnimation` for `BIO_ALERT` does `gotoAndStop(sIconID)`, plays the sound, and `GlobalFunc.SetText(BioAlertText.Alert_tf, sAlertText)`. It does **not** set a subtitle. `sAlertSubText` is shown only for an environment alert (`EnvAlertText.Subtext_tf`).
- No interface source contains the sentence "Cardiovascular / Respiratory / Circulatory", or "Cardio Respiratory Circulatory" as display text. **NOT FOUND** as a localized subtitle. The words in the symbol are Cardio, Respiratory, and Circulatory. "Cardiovascular" is not in the id.

Horizon's chip uses `sAlertSubText` when it is non-empty, unchanged. When it is empty, the subtitle is `hzPretty(icon)`. The `$1` text can only come from that fallback, so this alert's `sAlertSubText` was empty and the icon was the cardio symbol. The label that the old replace was written to produce, and that Flash would have produced, is `Cardio Respiratory Circulatory`. The same walk yields `Skeletal Muscular`, `Nervous System`, and `Digestive Immune`. Thermal, Radiation, Airborne, and Corrosive stay one word, as the early returns already did.

**Other replaces**

Every `.replace(` in the shipped movies and their `_lrg` twins was checked (HUD `horizon_block.as`, which is also `hudmenu_lrg`; Comms `hzc_block.as` and both `HUDMessagesMenu.as`; dialogue `hzd_block.as` and both `DialogueMenu.as`; scope `hzp_main.as` and both `ScopeMenu.as`; scanner `hzs_block.as` and both `MonocleMenu.as`). Replacements are `""`, `" "`, `"..."`, `"CTRL"`, `"O2"`, or a string variable. None use `$1`, `$2`, `$&`, `$$`, `` $` ``, `$'`, or a function.

`$$scanned`, `$$RANGE`, and `$$OUTPOST` in both Monocle movies are vanilla translation tokens passed to `GlobalFunc.SetText`. They are not `String.replace` replacements. Vanilla `BookMenu` does `.replace("$$MAIN_Font", "$MAIN_Font")`. That replacement is `$MAIN_Font` (`$` plus a letter), which is not a replacement code, and it is not our movie.

Older HUD decompiles still contain the old line and are not this build: `actionscript/HUDMenu_1.as`, `actionscript/HUDMenu_1.07.as`, `actionscript/imp/himp1/scripts/HUDMenu.as`, `actionscript/imp/himp1.07/scripts/HUDMenu.as`. They were not edited. Nothing in the repo compiles them.

**Change**

`hzSplitCamel` walks char codes and inserts a space when a lowercase letter (97-122) is followed by a capital (65-90). `hzPretty` calls that instead of `replace`.

## Harness

`tests/reaction_match_test.js` fails a shipped file when:

- a regex literal or a `new RegExp` pattern contains `\u` (the self-test probe `\u0041` is the one exception), `(?<`, `\p{`, or a `u` flag
- a `String.replace` replacement string contains `$` plus `$`, `&`, a backtick, a quote, or a digit
- that replacement is a function expression

`tests/planet_alert_test.js` pins `Cardio Respiratory Circulatory` and the literal Scaleform result `Cardi$1 $2espirator$1 $2irculatory`.
